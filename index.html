1. Information processed

Depending on your device, region, settings and the SDKs active in your version of the Game, the following categories may be processed by us or our service providers:

  • Gameplay and app activity: game launches, sessions, level or location progress, in-game interactions, tutorial steps, rewards and ad-related events.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system, app version, language, network-related information (including IP address), performance and diagnostic events, errors and crash information.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers where available and permitted, ad requests, ad views, clicks, rewarded-ad completions and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Approximate location: third parties may infer general location from an IP address for advertising, measurement or fraud prevention. We do not request precise GPS location for gameplay.
  • Local game data: progress and settings may be stored on your device to enable saved gameplay.

We do not require account registration and do not intentionally ask for your real name, personal email address, phone number, contacts or precise location as part of normal gameplay. If you email us, we process the information you choose to provide to respond to your request.

2. Why information is used

  • Operate the Game, save progress and deliver requested rewarded-ad benefits.
  • Understand gameplay, improve onboarding, balance and user experience through analytics.
  • Display, measure and, where legally permitted, personalize advertising.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems; protect against invalid ad traffic, abuse and fraud.
  • Respond to support and privacy inquiries and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where consent is legally required for a particular use, such as certain personalized advertising or identifier access, that use must be subject to the applicable consent and platform controls. This policy is not itself a consent request.

3. Advertising and analytics partners

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay advertising mediation, which may involve Unity Ads and Yandex Ads. Available demand sources can vary by region and version. We also use Unity Analytics to understand gameplay and improve the Game. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements.

  • Unity privacy policy — Unity Analytics, Unity Ads and LevelPlay-related services.
  • Yandex privacy policy — Yandex advertising services.
  • Google privacy policy — Android / Google Play platform services, as applicable.

Our advertising partners and their authorized demand partners may receive information needed for ad delivery, attribution, measurement, frequency control and fraud prevention. The published app-ads.txt identifies authorized advertising sellers; it is not a complete list of entities that receive user data.

We do not claim that device identifiers or advertising interaction data are always anonymous: they can be personal data under applicable law.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

You can manage or reset the Android advertising identifier and adjust available advertising privacy settings through your device settings. Depending on your region and the consent interface presented by the Game or its partners, you may also be able to accept, reject or change optional advertising purposes. Resetting an identifier or limiting personalization does not necessarily remove all advertisements.

Rewarded video ads are optional: you choose whether to watch an offered ad to receive the stated in-game reward. Core gameplay does not require watching every rewarded ad.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local game progress and settings may remain on your device until you clear the Game’s app data or uninstall it. Clearing app data can permanently remove saved progress. Third-party analytics and advertising providers maintain data according to their own retention policies and applicable obligations; their retention periods may differ.

For requests to access or delete information that we can reasonably identify and control, contact us using the email below. Please include the Game name and a description of your request, but do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents unless specifically and securely requested. We may need additional information to verify the request. Some device-level or partner-held records may need to be managed directly with the relevant provider. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

The Game does not currently require an in-game user account, so there is no account to delete.

6. Data security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network connections where supported, and limit access to information as reasonably necessary. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

The Game may be accessible to a general audience. A store age rating does not by itself determine whether a service is directed to children under applicable privacy law. We do not intentionally ask children to submit names or contact information through gameplay. Advertising and analytics practices for younger users must follow applicable laws, platform requirements and partner settings. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information, contact us.

8. International data processing

Advertising and analytics providers may process information on servers in countries other than your country of residence. Privacy protections may differ across jurisdictions. Transfers are subject to the providers’ applicable safeguards and legal obligations.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or information about personal data processing, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a relevant data protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights relating to information we control. We cannot directly change all information independently held by third-party providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its providers or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated policy here and revise the effective date. If a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide it as required.
